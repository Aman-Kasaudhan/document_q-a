# Nimbus Works Expense Policy

## Travel
Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Business class may be approved for flights longer than 6 hours with director approval. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 150 USD per night in standard cities and 220 USD per night in high-cost cities.

## Meals
The daily meal allowance during business travel is 60 USD. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense, together with receipts. Claims above 500 USD require manager approval. Approved claims are paid in the next monthly payroll run.
