# Nimbus Sync Product FAQ

## What is Nimbus Sync?
Nimbus Sync is a file synchronisation tool that keeps folders up to date across laptops, phones and servers.

## Plans and Pricing
The Free plan includes 5 GB of storage. The Pro plan costs 9 USD per month and includes 1 TB of storage. The Team plan costs 15 USD per user per month and includes 5 TB of shared storage and admin controls.

## Support
Support is available by email on weekdays from 09:00 to 18:00 UTC. Pro and Team customers receive a response within 24 hours. Free plan customers receive a response within 3 business days.

## Security and Recovery
Files are encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Deleted files can be restored from the trash for 30 days.

## File Limits
The maximum size of a single file is 50 GB on the Pro and Team plans and 2 GB on the Free plan.
