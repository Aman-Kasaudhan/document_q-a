# Nimbus Works Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 3-month probation may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Team leads decide the in-office days for their teams. Tuesday and Thursday are the default anchor days.

## Core Hours
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone. Meetings outside core hours need the agreement of all attendees.

## Equipment and Allowances
Remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of 400 USD. A monthly internet allowance of 30 USD is paid through payroll.

## Working from Abroad
Working from another country for more than 14 days per year requires approval from HR and Legal.
