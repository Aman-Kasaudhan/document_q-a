# Nimbus Works IT Security Policy

## Passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and changed every 90 days. Reuse of the last 12 passwords is not allowed. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all company accounts.

## Devices
Company laptops must use full-disk encryption. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the security team within 2 hours. Personal USB storage devices are prohibited on company laptops.

## Incident Reporting
Report suspected phishing emails with the Report Phish button in the mail client. The security team acknowledges every report within 1 business hour. Critical incidents are triaged within 30 minutes.

## Software
Only software from the approved catalogue may be installed. Requests for new software go through the IT service desk and are reviewed within 5 working days.
