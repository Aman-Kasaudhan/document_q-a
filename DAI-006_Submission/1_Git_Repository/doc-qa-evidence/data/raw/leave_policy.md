# Nimbus Works Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at 2 days per month. Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days. Unused sick leave does not carry over.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Requesting Leave
Submit leave requests in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance for absences longer than 5 days. Managers respond within 3 working days.
